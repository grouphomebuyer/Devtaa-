Who we are

A developer judged by what happens after handover

Devtaa Developers began in 1998 with a single eleven-flat building in Pune. Twenty-eight years later we have delivered 9.2 million square feet across six cities — and the measure we still care about most is how our earliest buildings are holding up today.

  • Forty-two projects delivered on or before the committed date
  • Five-year structural warranty and twenty-four months of on-site facility management
  • Every project RERA-registered, with published price sheets and no hidden loading
  • IGBC pre-certification pursued on every new development since 2019

Why Devtaa

Six promises we put in writing

Every one of these is written into the agreement for sale, not just into the brochure.

Design that ages well

We work with architects who plan for the twentieth year, not the launch weekend — cross-ventilation, daylight, and materials that wear gracefully.

Handed over on time

Forty-two of forty-two projects delivered on or before the committed possession date, including through two monsoon-hit construction cycles.

Transparent pricing

One price sheet, published charges, no hidden loading. What you see at the sales lounge is what appears in your agreement.

Engineered to last

Third-party structural audits at plinth, podium and topping-out, plus a five-year structural warranty on every home.

Green as standard

IGBC pre-certification, rainwater harvesting, STP-treated landscape irrigation and rooftop solar for common areas.

Service after handover

A dedicated facility management team stays on site for twenty-four months, and our helpdesk stays open for life.

Sustainability

Green buildings, not green brochures

We design for a thirty-year operating cost, not a launch-day price point. Lower running costs are the most honest measure of a sustainable building.

About Devtaa Developers

Twenty-eight years, six cities and one stubborn idea: a building should be judged in its twentieth year, not on its launch weekend.

Our story

From eleven flats in Kothrud to 9.2 million square feet

Devtaa Developers started as a contracting practice. That origin still shapes how we work: we came to development from the construction side, so drawings, materials and site sequencing are decided by people who have poured concrete themselves.

We have grown deliberately slowly. We take on four to six projects at a time and finish them before we take more land, which is why our delivery record has held through two downturns and a pandemic. We would rather turn down a parcel than stretch the team that has to build on it.

Today we build in Pune, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Nashik and — from 2026 — Delhi NCR, with 232 people on the payroll and a construction management team that has been together for over a decade.

Vision

Where we are going

To be the developer that Indian families recommend to their own children — because the building still works, the society still functions, and the resale value held.

Mission

How we get there

Design for daylight and ventilation before we design for the brochure. Publish one honest price. Build with third-party checks at every structural milestone. Hand over on the promised date, then stay on site until the snags are closed.

What we hold to

Four values, plainly stated

Do the boring things well

Waterproofing, drainage falls, expansion joints. Nobody photographs them; everybody lives with them.

The date is a promise

We publish a possession date in the agreement and staff the site to meet it, not to explain it away.

One number, in writing

No floor rise surprises, no ad-hoc infrastructure charges after booking.

Answer the phone

Our helpdesk stays open for the life of the building, long after the FM contract ends.

Milestones

Twenty-eight years, in order

Every entry here is a project handed over, not a launch announced.

  • 1998

    Devtaa Developers is founded in Pune with an eleven-flat building in Kothrud.

  • 2004

    First 100-home project delivered; the in-house structural and quality team is set up.

  • 2009

    Expansion into Nashik. One million square feet delivered in total.

  • 2014

    First Bengaluru project launched at Sarjapur Road.

  • 2019

    IGBC pre-certification becomes standard on every new development.

  • 2022

    Entry into Hyderabad; five million square feet delivered.

  • 2025

    9.2 million square feet delivered across five cities; NCR launch announced.

  • 2026

    Devtaa One breaks ground on the Dwarka Expressway — our first NCR address.

Community

The Devtaa Foundation

Two per cent of profit after tax goes to the Devtaa Foundation, which runs three programmes — all of them close to the work we do.

  • Skills for site workers — certified training in bar bending, shuttering and finishing, with placement across our contractor network
  • School infrastructure — sanitation blocks and drinking water systems at fourteen zilla parishad schools
  • Worker family support — creches and primary schooling at every Devtaa construction site

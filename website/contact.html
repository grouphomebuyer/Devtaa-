NRI corner

Buying from abroad

Our NRI desk handles the paperwork so that a purchase can be completed without a flight home.

  • Video site walkthroughs with the project engineer, scheduled to your time zone
  • Power of attorney drafting, attestation and registration support
  • Guidance on NRE/NRO remittance, TDS under section 195 and repatriation limits
  • Coordination with your chartered accountant for Form 15CA/15CB
  • Post-possession rental and property management through our facility team

Information is provided for guidance only and is not tax or legal advice. Please consult your own advisers.

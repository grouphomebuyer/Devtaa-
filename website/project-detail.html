Overview

Built around the courtyard, not the car park

Devtaa Aurum places all parking below a raised podium, which frees the entire ground plane for landscape. The four towers are set on the diagonal so that no apartment looks directly into another, and every home has cross-ventilation through at least two exposed faces.

The homes are planned with a wide living-dining span, a utility deck off the kitchen, and bedrooms sized for a real wardrobe rather than a rendered one. Ceiling height is 3.05m clear in living areas.

  • Podium-level landscape of 1.9 acres, zero cars at ground level
  • Vitrified tile flooring, branded CP and sanitary fittings, modular switches
  • Two service and two passenger lifts per tower, each with power backup
  • Five-year structural warranty, one-year snag-free service commitment

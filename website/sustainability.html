Our approach

Four things we do on every site

Water positive campuses

Rainwater harvesting sized to recharge more than the campus draws, with STP-treated water carrying the entire landscape and flushing load.

Low-energy buildings

Envelope-first design — shading, glazing ratio and insulated terraces — before any equipment is specified. Common-area load is 38% below baseline.

Materials with a shorter journey

Fly-ash blocks, GGBS-blended concrete and locally sourced aggregate. Seventy-one per cent of material value comes from within 400km.

Construction that behaves

Barricaded sites, sprinkler dust control, 100% construction-waste segregation and worker housing with power, water and sanitation.

Design first

The cheapest unit of energy is the one the building never needs

Most green certification is won with equipment. We try to win it with orientation.

  • Towers oriented to cut west-facing glazing to under 15% of the facade
  • Deep balconies and fins sized by shadow study, not by elevation drawing
  • Terrace insulation with reflective finish on every roof, not only the top floor
  • Every habitable room ventilated on at least two sides
  • Common-area lighting on daylight sensors, lifts with regenerative drives
  • Rooftop solar covering common-area load through daylight hours

People on site

Sustainability includes who builds it

A site that treats its workers badly does not produce good concrete. Ours run to a fixed standard, audited monthly.

  • Housing with electricity, running water, toilets and a kitchen for every site
  • Creche and primary schooling for workers' children on site
  • Mandatory safety induction, PPE issued and replaced free of charge
  • Wages paid directly to bank accounts, twice monthly, with no contractor deduction
  • Zero tolerance for anyone under eighteen on a Devtaa site

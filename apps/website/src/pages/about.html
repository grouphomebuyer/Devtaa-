Our story

A builder that starts with the daylight, not the drawing.

Mumbai builds fast. Too often it builds dark — deep flats where the sun reaches a metre past the window and stops.

Devtaa Developers took a different starting point. We design homes to interact with nature: louvered glass on the elevation lets sunlight through while cutting the harsh glare, so the home draws on daylight for most of the day instead of the switchboard. It is a small decision at the drawing stage that changes how a family lives in the flat for the next forty years.

That same instinct runs through everything else — the carpet area we quote, the timeline we commit to, the agreement we sign. We would rather sell one flat to a family that recommends us to three others than sell four to people who never call again.

The Devtaa promise

Six commitments, in writing, on every project.

01  RERA before sale

No unit is offered until the project carries a valid MahaRERA registration number, printed on every price sheet we issue.

02  Carpet area, as defined

We quote RERA carpet area. Built-up and super built-up numbers are never used to make a flat sound bigger than it is.

03  Bank-approved title

Our projects are funded and approved by scheduled banks, which means your home loan sanction is a formality, not a fight.

04  A dated possession commitment

The possession date in your agreement is the one we plan the construction schedule around — not a marketing figure.

05  Defect liability honoured

The five-year structural defect liability under RERA is not something we make you chase. Log it and we fix it.

06  One point of contact

The same relationship manager from your first site visit to your society handover. No transfers, no re-explaining.

The decision

When repairs stop making sense, redevelopment starts to.

A thirty-year-old building in Mumbai reaches a point where every monsoon costs the society more than the last, and the repair fund never quite recovers.

Redevelopment converts that liability into a larger, safer, lift-served home with a fresh structural life and a market value your members can actually realise. Done properly, it costs the existing members nothing.

  • A bigger home. Additional carpet area over your existing area, agreed in writing before you sign.
  • A corpus in hand. A lump sum to each member, paid as scheduled in the development agreement.
  • Rent through construction. Monthly rent, brokerage and shifting charges, so nobody is out of pocket while they wait.
  • A modern building. Lifts, covered parking, back-up power, CCTV, fire compliance and a fresh conveyance.
How we work

Seven steps, and you can stop at any of them.

Nothing binds your society until the general body votes. Everything before that is our cost and our risk.

01

First conversation

A meeting with your managing committee. We look at the plot, the existing built-up area and the members’ expectations. No documents needed yet.

02

Free feasibility study

Our architects and legal team assess FSI and TDR potential, the applicable DCPR provisions and the achievable carpet gain. You get it in writing, whether or not you appoint us.

03

Offer to the general body

Carpet area offered, corpus, rent, timeline and penalties — presented to every member, not negotiated behind a committee door.

04

Appointment and agreement

If members vote us in, we execute the development agreement and individual PAAAs, registered, with a Section 79A-compliant process throughout.

05

Approvals and MahaRERA

IOD, CC and MahaRERA registration obtained before demolition. Members receive copies of every sanction.

06

Vacating and construction

Rent, brokerage and shifting charges paid before you hand over the keys. Monthly progress updates to a members’ group through construction.

07

Possession and conveyance

OC received, homes handed over, new society formed and conveyance executed. Then the five-year defect liability period runs, and we answer the phone.

Self-redevelopment too. If your society would rather retain the development profit and build it yourselves, we can act as project management consultants instead of developers — same team, different mandate. Ask us which route suits your plot.

What makes a redevelopment work

Building age

Typically 25 years and older, or any structure declared dilapidated by the municipal corporation.

Plot size

Plots from roughly 800 sq.m. upwards, though smaller plots can work where the road width allows the FSI.

Member consent

A clear majority in favour under the Section 79A directives. We will not start a project a society is split over.

Clean paperwork

Registered society, property card and conveyance in order — and where they are not, we will help you fix them first.

Overview

The building that set our standard.

Devtaa Desire is a single 22-storey tower carrying 109 homes on H. D. Koparkar Marg in Nahur East — a five-minute pocket between the Central line and LBS Marg, with Bhandup and Mulund on either side.

The brief was a difficult one: build a 392 sq.ft. 1 BHK that a family of three could actually live in. The answer was to stop treating a compact home as a scaled-down large one. Every square foot in Desire was planned for a use — a dry balcony that keeps the utility out of the kitchen, a living room shaped for a sofa and a dining table rather than one or the other, and louvered glazing that pulls daylight the full depth of the flat.

The project is complete and homes are ready to move into. Resale and remaining developer inventory both come through our sales desk, so you get the same paperwork discipline either way.

Who we are

We are not only in the business of building homes — we build lifelong relationships.

Devtaa Developers Pvt. Ltd. is a Mumbai-based real estate firm working across the development, marketing and sale of affordable-luxury homes, residential communities and society redevelopment.

Our design brief starts with something most buildings in Mumbai give away too easily: daylight. We plan our elevations with louvered glazing that lets the sun in without the glare, so a Devtaa home stays naturally lit through the day — and naturally cooler for it.

  • Every project registered with MahaRERA before a single unit is sold.
  • Carpet areas stated exactly as RERA defines them — no loading, no surprises.
  • Specialists in self-redevelopment and society redevelopment mandates.
  • Homes priced for real Mumbai families, finished to a standard they are proud of.

Why Devtaa

Four things we refuse to compromise on

Light-first design

Louvered glazing and planned orientation so daylight reaches deep into every home, not just the balcony.

Clean title, clear RERA

Registered project numbers, disclosed timelines and a written agreement before any payment is taken.

Honest carpet area

The number on our price sheet is the number RERA defines. What you measure is what you bought.

Relationships, not sales

Most of our buyers come from a referral by an earlier one. That is the only marketing metric we track.

For housing societies

Redevelopment, run the way your society deserves.

If your building is thirty years old and the repair bills have stopped making sense, redevelopment is a decision your members will live with for the next fifty. We treat it that way.

  • Free feasibility study — FSI potential, carpet gain and corpus, in writing.
  • Transparent offer sheet presented to the full general body, not just a committee.
  • Rent, brokerage and shifting charges paid on schedule through construction.
  • MahaRERA registration and bank-approved title before work begins.

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Devtaa Developers Pvt. Ltd. does with the information you give us through this website.

What we collect

Only what you type into an enquiry form: your name, mobile number, email address, the configuration or project you are interested in, and any message you write. We also collect ordinary technical information that every website receives — your browser type, the pages you visited and the approximate region you visited from.

Why we collect it

  • To call, message or email you back about the enquiry you made.
  • To send you the price sheet, floor plans or project information you asked for.
  • To arrange a site visit and follow up on it.
  • To keep a record of the enquiry for our own sales and compliance purposes.

Consent and DNC/NDNC

When you tick the consent box on an enquiry form, you authorise us and our authorised representatives to contact you by phone, WhatsApp, SMS and email in connection with that enquiry, even if your number is registered on the Do Not Call or National Do Not Call registry. You can withdraw that consent at any time by writing to us, and we will stop.

Who we share it with

Our own sales team, and where relevant the channel partner who introduced you. We do not sell, rent or trade your contact details to third parties. Where a project is jointly developed, the co-developer’s sales team may also contact you about that project.

How long we keep it

For as long as needed to serve your enquiry and to meet our legal and accounting obligations. If you ask us to delete your details and we are not required to retain them, we will.

Your rights

You can ask us what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or withdraw your consent to being contacted. Write to privacy@devtaadevelopers.com and we will respond within a reasonable period.

Cookies

This website does not set advertising cookies of its own. If we later add analytics or advertising tags, this section will be updated to say which ones and why.

Security

Enquiry data is transmitted over an encrypted connection and stored with access limited to the people who need it. No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim otherwise — but we do not ask for anything on this website that we do not need.

Terms of Use

Information on this site

Everything on this website is provided for general information. It does not constitute an offer, an invitation to offer, or a contract of any kind. Nothing here creates a binding obligation on Devtaa Developers Pvt. Ltd.

Images and plans

All images, elevations, layouts, landscaping and amenity depictions on this website are artist’s impressions and are indicative of design intent only. They are not to scale and are not a representation of the finished product. Actual construction is governed by the sanctioned plans and the agreement for sale.

Prices and areas

Prices marked with an asterisk are indicative starting prices, exclude GST, stamp duty, registration and other statutory charges, and are subject to revision without notice. Carpet area is stated as defined under Section 2(k) of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016. Availability changes; confirm the current position with our sales desk before relying on any figure.

RERA

Project registration numbers are stated on the respective project pages. Full details of each registered project, including approved plans and quarterly progress, are available on the MahaRERA portal at maharera.maharashtra.gov.in. You are advised to verify all particulars independently before making a booking.

Third-party links

Where this website links to another site, we are not responsible for that site’s content or its privacy practices.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Mumbai have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising from them.

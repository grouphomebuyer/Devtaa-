Overview

A building the neighbourhood already trusted, rebuilt.

Devtaa Vijay CHS is our redevelopment mandate in Bhandup East — the project where the members of an ageing society traded a structure that had outlived its repairs for a new one, at no cost to themselves.

Redevelopment buyers get something an outer-suburb launch cannot offer: a plot that is already part of a working neighbourhood. The roads, the water connection, the market, the station and the schools were all there before the building was. What changes is the structure — lifts, covered parking, back-up power, fire compliance, and homes planned for how a family in Bhandup actually lives.

The sale component carries 1 and 2 BHK homes in the ₹96.20 Lakh to ₹1.63 Crore band. Availability is limited and moves with the construction stage, so speak to the sales desk for the live position rather than planning around this page.

Overview

A Goregaon address that finally has room to breathe.

Devtaa Pratham is a redevelopment at Pragati Nagar, off M.G. Road, opposite Aquinas International School — one of the few pockets of Goregaon West where a new building could be planned around open space instead of squeezed between two others.

The tower carries 2 and 3 BHK homes of 675 to 800 sq.ft. RERA carpet, laid out so the living room and the master bedroom both take an external face. Louvered glazing on the elevation lets the sun in through the day and keeps the glare and the heat out — the same detail that runs through every Devtaa building.

Because this is a redevelopment, the plot is already served by the city: water, drainage, roads and a working neighbourhood, not an outer-suburb promise. Possession is committed for December 2026 under the MahaRERA registration.

Layouts

Planned around the outside wall

Both the living room and the master bedroom take an external face, so the two rooms a family actually lives in get the daylight and the cross ventilation — not the corridor.

  • Living and dining planned as one usable span, not two awkward halves.
  • Dry balcony off the kitchen, so the utility area stays out of the living space.
  • Bedrooms sized for a queen bed and a wardrobe wall, measured before it was drawn.
  • Full-height louvered glazing on the external face for light without glare.
